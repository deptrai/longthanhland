- **Total Investment**: 17.32B VND
- **Total Revenue (3 years)**: 16.85B VND
- **Net Loss (3 years)**: -470M VND

### High-Impact Risks

**Risk 1: Funding Shortfall**
- Impact: Critical
- Probability: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Secure Series A early (Month 10)
  - Revenue-based financing
  - Strategic partnerships
  - Cost optimization

**Risk 2: Competition from Batdongsan/Chợ Tốt**
- Impact: High
- Probability: High
- Mitigation:
  - Focus on Long Thành niche
  - AI differentiation
  - Better UX
  - Agent partnerships

**Risk 3: Slow User Adoption**
- Impact: High
- Probability: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Aggressive marketing
  - Referral program
  - Agent incentives
  - Free tier

**Risk 4: Technical Scalability Issues**
- Impact: High
- Probability: Low
- Mitigation:
  - Cloud infrastructure
  - Load testing
  - Monitoring
  - DevOps team

**Risk 5: Team Turnover**
- Impact: Medium
- Probability: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Competitive salaries

  - Career growth

**Risk 6: Regulatory Changes**
- Impact: Medium
- Probability: Low
